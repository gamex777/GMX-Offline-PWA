This package includes EmulatorJS 4.2.3 and selected libretro WebAssembly cores.
Project: https://github.com/EmulatorJS/EmulatorJS
Cores bundled: fceumm, snes9x, gambatte, mgba, parallel_n64,
desmume2015, genesis_plus_gx.

No BIOS files or commercial game ROMs are included. Use only ROMs and BIOS files
that you are legally entitled to use.
